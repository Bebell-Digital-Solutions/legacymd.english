Frequently Asked Questions

We answer your questions about how developing your Digital Practice works.

A digital practice is a professional environment designed for health, wellness, and personalized service specialists to manage their care digitally. It allows you to showcase services, receive requests, schedule appointments, communicate with patients, and organize processes without relying on manual or in-person management. At LegacyMD, we develop digital practices tailored to each specialty to optimize care, time, and the professional's organization.

1- Health Professionals: General practitioners and specialists, psychologists, therapists, nutritionists, physiotherapists.

2- Consultants and Coaches: Professionals who offer private sessions or advisory services.

3- Medical Centers and Clinics: Practices with one or several specialists.

4- Wellness Services: Alternative therapies, holistic health, and emotional well-being.

Our solution allows you to organize your schedule, reduce cancellations, centralize information about your services, and facilitate communication with patients. It also improves the care experience, optimizes operational time, and helps attract new patients in a structured way.

Our team provides guidance during the development, configuration, and use of the digital practice. We offer technical support, guidance, and necessary adjustments to ensure stable and clear operation through email, WhatsApp, and tickets depending on your plan.

The security and confidentiality of information is a priority. We apply data protection best practices, including SSL certificates on your domain, to guarantee responsible handling of professional and patient information.
